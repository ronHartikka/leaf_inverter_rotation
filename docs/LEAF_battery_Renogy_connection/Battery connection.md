# Battery connection  
  
****	position inverter near center of leaf inverter****  
****	inverter should be off - not on or remote****  
****	car should be off - drawing as little power as possible  when  connecting inverter to battery****  
  
	12 mm for +terminal, 13 mm for -terminal  
  
	socket is best  
	careful not to let clamp spin on battery post - it wants to  
	connect negative (black wire)  first - that way fewer places to make connection by accident  
	then connect the positive  (red wire)  from inverter to battery  
	then turn on the car  
	check that the following are off  
  
    - [ ] ****Audio****  
    - [ ] ****Heated seats****  
    - [ ] ****HVAC****  
    - [ ] ****Heated steering wheel****  
    - [ ] ****Center display****  
    - [ ] ****Headlights (switch OFF, not AUTO)****  
  
	make sure no load is plugged into the inverter  
	turn on the inverter - it beeps once and an led comes on  
	plug a test load (light) into the inverter  
  
No loads should be connected to ground  
The furnace is so make sure the green wire from the inverter doesn’t get to it  
  
  
Black wire belonging to furnace  
	remove from normally load side of switch mounted to the furnace  
	use wire nut to connect black wire of temporary cord to it  
  
White wire belonging to furnace  
	remove from wire nut inside box that is mounted INSIDE the furnace  
	use wire nut to connect white wire of temporary cord to it  
  
Hood of car  
	close it as far as possible with out resting it on the inverter  
	a piece of 2x blocking the latch is a good thickness though this is not storm proof
